# Open Images → EcoTrace Laptop Pilot — Manual Visual QA Report

Status: Manual-review checkpoint (human visual QA)
Sprint: P4.2 dataset acquisition — Laptop conversion pilot
Scope: `laptop` class only — 21 staged Open Images V7 images, 35 converted boxes
Audience: dataset engineers, annotation reviewers, QA leads

---

> **What this report is.** A per-image human visual inspection of every converted
> annotation in the pilot, with a per-image ACCEPT / REVIEW / REJECT decision and a
> single pilot verdict. It reports **only observed counts** — no quality score,
> accuracy percentage, or dataset-readiness metric is invented. It marks **nothing**
> as READY or RELEASED. No source image, converted label, converter, or frozen
> pipeline module was modified to produce it.

> **How to reproduce the previews.** Annotated previews and the contact sheet were
> generated by the manual-review tool
> `dataset_acquisition/staging/openimages_laptop_v1/manual_review/make_visual_qa.py`

> `device_ai.dataset.metadata.blur_score`). Artifacts:

## 8. Pilot verdict

### `PILOT_PASS_WITH_REVIEW`

Rationale: 16 of 21 images are clean ACCEPTs with correct, tight, correctly-classed
`laptop` boxes. The pilot becomes fully usable after a **small, bounded** follow-up:

1. **Remove 1 image** — QA14 (`79182035199f2b58`): keyboard-only macro, blurry, and
   confusable with the separate `keyboard` class. Not a usable `laptop` exemplar.
2. **Review/repair 4 images** before ingestion:
   - QA03 — split the group box into per-laptop boxes (or drop).
   - QA04 — add the missing prominent laptop; tighten the front box.
   - QA15 — tighten the loose box to the laptop only.
   - QA01 — confirm the borderline-blur low-light example is worth keeping.

The conversion pipeline itself showed **no implementation defect**: every problem
above originates in the **source Open Images annotations** (group boxes, non-exhaustive
labelling, an unsuitable macro), not in the converter. The converter reproduced the
source boxes faithfully and correctly (formulas, class id, provenance all verified in
the conversion sprint). **No converter or frozen-pipeline change is warranted.**

## 9. Next-stage operations (out of scope for this checkpoint — not performed here)

These are recorded for the follow-up sprint and were deliberately **not** executed
during this QA (no files renamed, nothing committed, no dataset marked ready):

- **Canonical filename rename.** Ingestion into a named EcoTrace collection will
  require renaming the provenance-preserved OID hash stems to the
  `<class>_<source>_<seq>.<ext>` convention (this is exactly the expected
  `FILENAME_CONVENTION` failure from P4.2.1). This must be a **separate** operation
  that records a `stem → canonical` mapping in provenance so traceability is kept.
  Do **not** rename the source-preserved staging images as part of QA.
- **Apply the QA decisions**: drop QA14; re-annotate/repair QA03, QA04, QA15;
  reviewer sign-off on QA01.
- **Then, and only then**, consider scaling acquisition + conversion to the
  remaining taxonomy classes and Dataset v1.0 assembly (governed separately by
  `docs/ai/dataset_v1_freeze_policy.md` and
  `docs/engineering/dataset_v1_release.md`).

> **STOP.** This checkpoint ends with this report and the contact sheet. Do not scale
> to other classes, rename staged images, or assemble a dataset release until these
> results are reviewed.
